# Games

## Hide and seek — `/verstecken`

One animal's picture appears; find that animal in the living tank and tap it.
**Not one word on screen.** A 👀 button makes the target sparkle when stuck.

Two things make it fair: [[The Cast#Telling them apart]] guarantees same-kind
animals are distinguishable, and anything under 14px is excluded from the pool.

## The maze — `/labyrinth`

A coral maze she drags a seahorse through to reach a crown. Walls block but
never punish — the swimmer slides along them. No timer, no way to lose.
Generated by recursive backtracking with a few extra openings so a wrong turn
is never a long dead end.

## The maker — `/machen`

Build a creature: body, three colour slots, size, sparkle, a dice for a
surprise, and an optional name. It swims in the tank with the book cast and
follows her to every place.

## Find game — `/spiel`

The original: a name and four portraits. It **requires reading**, so the name is
now spoken aloud and the card can be tapped to hear it again. Hide and seek is
the wordless alternative.

## Typing — `/tippen`

A creature appears and she types its name — first letter, or the whole word.
Two clearly-lit modes, because a single ambiguous toggle read as broken.

## The map — `/karte`

A sea chart of [[The Nine Places]]. Each place is a porthole showing its own
water; routes bow like currents; gold currents flow to where she can go.
Unreachable places stay visible but quiet.

## The tilt game

Only on a device with a motion sensor. Tilt to roll pearls into the chest.
